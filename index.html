How has your partner been doing lately?

You are here because something has felt off and you are trying to understand what you are seeing. That instinct matters. This is a quiet space to share what you have noticed - honestly, without judgement, and without it having to be perfectly formed.

This takes about five minutes.

Your answers are saved privately on this device. You can stop and come back at any point.

At the end you will get a clearer picture of what might be happening - and what, if anything, you might want to do next. That part is always your choice.

A lot of partners notice something is wrong before the father does. They watch, they worry, they wait - and often they have no language for what they are seeing and no one to ask. This tool exists because that should not have to happen.

Built by Sarah Bailey, Founder of Parents2Be International. NHS eLfH Perinatal Mental Health trained. This is not a clinical diagnosis. It is a starting point for getting the right support.

Before you begin

You are not alone in noticing this

You are here because you care about him - and because something in you has been quietly wondering whether what you are seeing is more than tiredness or stress.

What you are noticing matters. Partners are often the first to see the signs - long before a father would name them himself. This assessment gives you a way to put words to what you have been observing.

Your responses will not go in anyone's medical record, will not be shared without your permission, and will not affect any decisions about your family.

This is for you, so you can help him.
